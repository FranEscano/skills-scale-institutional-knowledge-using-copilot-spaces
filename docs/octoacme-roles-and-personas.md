# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designers and User Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and User Researchers ensure that solutions are usable, accessible, and grounded in customer needs. They translate research and design insights into clear experience requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, usability testing, and accessibility reviews
- Create workflows, wireframes, prototypes, and interaction specifications
- Define usability goals and validate designs against user needs
- Document design decisions, constraints, and edge cases
- Collaborate on acceptance criteria and usability-focused quality checks

### Goals
- Improve usability, accessibility, and customer satisfaction
- Reduce rework caused by unclear or unvalidated user experience decisions
- Establish a consistent, evidence-based customer experience

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Product Managers to translate customer insights into prioritized requirements
- Collaborate with Developers on feasibility and implementation details
- Work with QA/Testing and stakeholders to validate usability and acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Leads and Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide the technical approach, architecture, and integration decisions that enable reliable delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Define and communicate technical direction and architectural standards
- Review designs, integration points, and cross-system dependencies
- Help estimate technical work and identify implementation risks
- Promote maintainability, observability, and appropriate engineering practices
- Support incident analysis and technical trade-off decisions

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and supportable solutions
- Identify technical risks and dependencies early
- Improve consistency across systems and teams

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Developers on design reviews, implementation, and estimates
- Communicate technical constraints and trade-offs to Product Managers
- Work with Project Managers to identify risks, dependencies, and delivery impacts
- Coordinate with DevOps/SRE and Security/Privacy representatives on operational and control requirements

---

## DevOps/Release Engineers and SREs

### Role Summary
DevOps/Release Engineers and Site Reliability Engineers provide the operational, deployment, monitoring, and release practices needed to deliver and support changes safely.

### Responsibilities
- Build and maintain CI/CD pipelines and deployment automation
- Assess release readiness and coordinate deployment execution
- Define monitoring, alerting, capacity, and service-level expectations
- Prepare rollback and recovery procedures
- Participate in incident response and operational improvement activities

### Goals
- Improve deployment reliability and lead time
- Detect and reduce operational risk
- Enable fast, safe recovery from failures

### Typical Communication
- Coordinate release plans and operational risks with Project Managers
- Partner with Developers on build, deployment, and observability requirements
- Work with QA/Testing on smoke and end-to-end release verification
- Collaborate with Security/Privacy representatives on secure pipelines and controls
- Coordinate support readiness with Customer Support/Operations representatives

---

## Security and Privacy Representatives

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Representatives help teams identify and manage security, privacy, compliance, and regulatory risks throughout delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements for security and privacy obligations
- Advise on threat modeling, secure design, and required safeguards
- Support security scanning and risk remediation planning
- Validate that applicable controls and evidence requirements are addressed
- Escalate unacceptable or unresolved risks through the defined escalation path

### Goals
- Reduce exposure to security and privacy risks
- Embed required controls into planning and implementation
- Enable informed, documented risk decisions

### Typical Communication
- Partner with Product Managers to clarify requirements and acceptable risk
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers on secure implementation
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on security and privacy validation
- Help DevOps/Release and Project Managers incorporate controls and review gates into delivery plans

---

## Customer Support and Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations Representatives represent operational readiness and the needs of customers who use or support a released feature.

### Responsibilities
- Gather support, operational, and customer-facing requirements
- Define and validate support documentation, known issues, escalation paths, and runbooks
- Identify operational training, staffing, or tooling needs
- Coordinate customer communications and feedback after release
- Surface recurring customer issues and improvement opportunities

### Goals
- Improve release readiness and customer adoption
- Reduce support burden and resolution time
- Ensure feedback from operations and customers informs future priorities

### Typical Communication
- Work with Product Managers to communicate customer needs and feedback
- Coordinate operational readiness and release communications with Project Managers
- Partner with Developers and DevOps/SRE on support tooling, observability, and known issues
- Collaborate with UX/UI roles to identify experience problems and improvement opportunities
